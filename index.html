WHY US

01

Prime and Connected Location

Situated along Mbaazi Road, the development provides a quiet, leafy environment while remaining minutes away from Valley Arcade, Junction Mall, top schools, hospitals, and business hubs like Westlands and the Nairobi CBD.

02

Resort-Style Amenities & Free Fittings

Set across a 1.1-acre gated community, the three 17-floor towers include a swimming pool, clubhouse, library, children’s play area, borehole, full backup generator, and over 420 parking spaces. Units also come with complimentary fittings such as fitted burners, a water purifier, and a water heater.

03

Strong Investment Value & Flexible Terms

Units start at an accessible off-plan price of KES 7.6 million (with options ranging from 1 to 3 bedrooms). Buyers can secure a property with a 20% deposit and pay the remaining balance in flexible installments leading up to the project completion date in December 2027, promising high rental demand and capital appreciation.
